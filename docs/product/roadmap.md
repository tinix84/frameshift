# Roadmap

The roadmap is outcome-based. Dates are intentionally absent until maintainers establish delivery capacity.

## M0 — Contract foundation (P0)

Exit criteria:

- Canonical session, graph, capability, engine-result, and checkpoint schemas validate.
- Reference fixture runs without provider SDKs.
- Hashes are stable across supported environments.
- Codex, Claude Code, and generic adapter bootstraps map into one capability profile.

## M1 — Framing vertical slice (P0/P1)

Exit criteria:

- A user can classify and correct an intake.
- A why/how ladder creates three candidate frames with explicit boundaries.
- A human must approve the working frame.
- The session resumes from a deterministic checkpoint in a second runtime.

## M2 — Causal evidence graph (P1)

Exit criteria:

- Users edit typed nodes and edges.
- Multiple causal hypotheses coexist with supporting and contradicting evidence.
- The system recommends discriminating evidence without claiming certainty.
- Graph merges surface conflicts rather than silently resolving them.

## M3 — Options and decisions (P1/P2)

Exit criteria:

- Options span component, system, operational, supply-chain, and business levers where relevant.
- Hard constraints filter explicitly; preferences remain visible.
- Comparison includes uncertainty and sensitivity.
- Decision records capture approval, dissent, assumptions, and validation plan.

## M4 — Collaborative alpha (P2)

Exit criteria:

- Workspace roles and policy-controlled tools are enforced.
- Retention, redaction, deletion, and audit export are tested.
- A small set of engineering teams completes benchmark scenarios and usability interviews.

## Later possibilities (P3)

- Domain packs for automotive, manufacturing, software incidents, and product strategy.
- Simulation and requirements-tool integrations.
- Real-time workshop facilitation and graph collaboration.
- Empirical calibration of confidence and option-diversity measures.

These are not commitments. Evidence from the vertical slices should determine sequencing.
