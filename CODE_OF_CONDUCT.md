# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FrameShift a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, show empathy, and distinguish critiques of ideas from critiques of people. Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers will review reports promptly, respect reporter privacy, and take proportionate corrective action. Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed from the project.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
