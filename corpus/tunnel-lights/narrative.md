# Tunnel lights

This synthetic case is inspired by the problem ownership theme of *Are Your
Lights On?* by Gerald M. Weinberg and Donald C. Gause. The tunnel, panel, and
reminder details below are invented for this fixture.

The request is to add a reminder mechanism to a tunnel lighting subsystem. The
first prototype creates a new failure: repeated maintenance prompts fill the
subsystem panel and obscure the active fault signal that operators need to
see. The problem is reminder behaviour and fault visibility at the named
subsystem level.

Moving upward to a product or business frame would change the owner and
decision being examined without evidence that the broader outcome is the
problem. The useful reframe stays at the subsystem level and asks for the
missing display priority, reminder frequency, and fault visibility measure
before proposing a mechanism. The downstream effect to investigate is whether
operators can still identify an active lighting fault during the reminder
sequence.
