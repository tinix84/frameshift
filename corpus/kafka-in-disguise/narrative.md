# Kafka in disguise

## Request as it arrived

“We should use Kafka.”

## Why it misleads

The request names a technology, but it does not say what outcome should change,
what is failing today, or who owns the decision. Treating Kafka as the problem
would optimise a proposed mechanism before the problem is understood.

## Reframe

Classify the request as a proposal and ask the owner to name the outcome. The
engine may show the abstraction ladder stopping at the arriving mechanism, but
it must not invent an outcome or approve the technology.

## Downstream change

Until the outcome, current evidence, and accountable owner are supplied, later
causal analysis, option comparison, and decision approval remain open. The next
checkpoint is intake correction.
