# Bracket weight mandate

This synthetic case is inspired by the problem ownership theme of *Are Your
Lights On?* by Gerald M. Weinberg and Donald C. Gause. The supplier, bracket,
and vehicle details are invented for this fixture.

A supplier engineer has been told to remove mass from a mounting bracket. The
engineer can frame the part's mass and strength trade-off, but the higher
vehicle payload and customer outcome belong to the customer. The downstream
effect to investigate is whether the bracket still meets its specified loads
after mass is removed; vehicle payload is a separate customer decision.

The correct move is therefore to reframe within the component boundary and
record the customer-owned frame as out of scope rather than proposing it.
